Frequently asked questions

Useful information before downloading the app.

What is Fata?

Fata is a secure chat app for Android, built with Flutter. You can send messages, images, videos and audio, and record voice messages.

What can I send in Fata?

You can send text messages, images, videos and audio files, and you can record and send voice messages.

Why can't I download the APK?

Check that your internet connection is working. Google Drive may also show a confirmation screen or temporarily limit downloads. If the problem continues, try again later.

Why does Android show an installation warning?

Android warns you about apps installed outside Google Play. Make sure you downloaded the APK from this official page before proceeding, and never ignore a security warning without understanding it.

Do I need internet to use Fata?

Yes. Fata is a chat app, so you need an internet connection to send and receive messages and media.
